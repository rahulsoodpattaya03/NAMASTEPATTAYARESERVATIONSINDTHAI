Thailand Visa — Quick Guide (Indian passport)

  • • Visa on Arrival / Exemption: rules change often — many Indian passport holders can enter for short tourism stays under current schemes.
  • • Tourist Visa (TR): for longer leisure stays.
  • • Common documents: passport valid 6+ months, passport photos, return/onward ticket, proof of accommodation, bank statement / funds proof, completed application.

⚠️ Visa rules change frequently. This is general guidance only — always confirm current requirements with the Royal Thai Embassy/Consulate or a licensed visa agent before travel.
