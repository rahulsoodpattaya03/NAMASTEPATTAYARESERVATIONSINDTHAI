1. Who we are

Namaste Pattaya Reservations ("we") runs this app to help visitors book venues and services in Pattaya. We are the data controller under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (PDPA). Company: [registered company name and address]. Contact: [email] · WhatsApp [number].

2. What we collect

  • Account: email, password (stored encrypted by our login provider), name if you give it.
  • Bookings and orders: name, phone number, date, time, number of guests, notes, and for delivery your address and, only if you tap the location button, your GPS location.
  • Partners (venues): business name, contact name, phone, email, venue photo, staff names, roles, phone numbers and shifts entered by the venue.
  • Meet new people / Friends Location: the profile, messages and location you choose to share in those features.
  • Chats with Raju (AI guide): the messages you send.
  • App use: anonymous counts (for example "a booking was sent"), without your name or contact details.

3. Why we use it

  • To send your booking or order to the venue and let them contact you (to perform our service to you).
  • To show your order status, including through Raju.
  • To use your location for a delivery or the Friends Location feature, only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • To keep the app safe, prevent misuse and meet legal duties.
  • To improve the app using anonymous statistics.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use it for advertising.

4. Who receives it

  • The venue you book, and its staff who use our restaurant dashboard, only for your booking.
  • Our service providers: Supabase (database and login), Vercel (website hosting) and Google Gemini (answers from Raju). Some of their servers are outside Thailand. [Lawyer: confirm cross-border transfer safeguards under PDPA Sections 28–29.]
  • Authorities, if Thai law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

Bookings and orders: [e.g. 2 years] after the booking date. Accounts: until you delete them. Chats with Raju: [period]. Anonymous statistics may be kept longer.

6. Your rights

You can ask to see, copy, correct or delete your data, object to or limit its use, and withdraw consent. Contact us at [email]. You can also complain to Thailand's Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC).

7. Age

Meet new people is for adults 18+ only. The app is not directed at children.

8. Security

Data is protected with access rules so that each venue sees only its own orders. If a data breach creates a risk to you, we will notify the authority and, where required, you.

9. Changes

We will post any changes on this page with a new date.
